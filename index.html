// Design Process

How I turn problems into products

Every engagement follows a structured, research-backed process — so you always know what's happening and why.

01 — Discover
Research & Define

User interviews, competitive analysis, heuristic evaluations. I build understanding before touching a single wireframe.

02 — Define
IA & Flows

Sitemaps, user journey maps, task flows, and information architecture — the invisible skeleton every great product needs.

03 — Design
Wireframe → Hi-Fi

Lo-fi to hi-fi in Figma with complete component libraries, auto-layout grids, and annotated specs ready for dev handoff.

04 — Validate
Test & Iterate

Prototype testing with real users, A/B test analysis, and post-launch metric reviews. The work doesn't end at handoff.

// Featured Case Study

UniConnect: Designing for students, by a student

A closer look at how I approached designing an academic mobile portal from scratch — applying UX research methodology learned in school to a real-world problem I personally experience.

View Full Project →
01

User Research

Surveyed 15 fellow students to identify pain points in existing academic portals — slow navigation and confusing grade views topped the list.

02

Wireframing & Iteration

Sketched 30+ screens, moved to lo-fi in Figma, iterated 3 times based on peer feedback before reaching hi-fi prototype stage.

03

Final Prototype & Outcome

Delivered a clickable hi-fi prototype with 24 screens. Received an A+ grade and commendation from my professor for thoroughness in user testing.
